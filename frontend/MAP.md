# The frontend's map

What lives where under `frontend/`, the DOM contract the checks select on, and
the tests by file. Paths are relative to `frontend/`; a brief cites this map by
section. Rules and reasons live in the records, open items in `design/pending.md`.

Every module under `src/lib/` but `fixtures.ts` is pure: data in, data out,
no DOM, no stores, nothing measured. DOM-touching code lives under
`src/canvas/` and `src/frame/`: `size.ts` measures text, `Canvas.svelte` draws
the level, the frame's components draw the panes; `layout.ts` is DOM-free but
async and bound to ELK, `router.ts` DOM-free and synchronous, `glyphs.ts` and
`palette.ts` data. `src/live/socket.ts`
alone touches `WebSocket`. The stores live under `src/state/`, Svelte runes in
`.svelte.ts` modules; `prefs.svelte.ts` alone touches local storage, and its
`parsePrefs` is pure; `session.svelte.ts` alone touches `history` and writes
the hash, which `App.svelte` also reads on `popstate`. `rekey.ts` is generic
over the layout's type, so `src/lib/` never imports `src/canvas/`. The panes
receive what they show and call back what they change; only `Frame.svelte`
knows where they sit, and only `App.svelte` wires
them to the stores. The code imports the generated types by name from
`./descriptor`.

## Vocabulary

- A **face** is `{path, side, port}`: a node's path, the side it is declared
  on, its name. A root input is `("", "in", name)`; at a primitive root it is
  also the primitive's own input face. A wire endpoint on the level's own path is
  one of the level's boundary faces (`wireFaces`).
- A **terminal producer** is a primitive output or a root input; a
  **terminal consumer** a primitive input or a root output.
- A terminal producer's **chain** lists the producer faces the walk passes,
  the terminal last. An output face is its own first producer; a consumer, a
  primitive input or an assembly input face, starts at what feeds it. Its length is
  what the brief counts as hops.
- A **route** is rooted at the terminal producer: its consumers, every wire it
  crosses with the wire's level, and the set of levels. Any face on it, and
  any wire, gives the same route. A **traced** route (`Traced`) carries its
  **origin**, the face it was traced from: a pin's or a port's own face, a
  wire's producer face (`producerFace`); the status line and the origin's
  halo name it, the drawing is the producer's whole tree. The session's
  **standing route** is the origin alone, the route recomputed from the model.
- A **block**, the code's word where a reader's text says "component", is an
  `InPin`, an `OutPin` or a `ChildBlock`; `role` tells which, a child's its
  class. Pin ids are `⟨in⟩name` and `⟨out⟩name`, a child's id its path, a
  port's id `block|side|name`.
- A level wire's **id** is its consumer's port id, unique within a level.
- A **view** is `{x, y, scale}`: the world point at the SVG's top-left corner
  and the screen pixels per world unit. With the viewport's size it gives the
  world rectangle the SVG shows, its `viewBox` (`viewRect`). `zoomAt` and `pan`
  take screen pixels relative to the SVG.
- A **target** is what a click selects, `{kind, id}`: a block or pin by block
  id, a wire by wire id, a port square by port id, a canvas region by its
  folder's path (kind `folder`). `Canvas.svelte` exports the type from its
  module script.
- A **selection** is `{level, target}` (`Selected` in `detail.ts`); the level
  is where it was made, since pin and wire ids repeat across levels, a folder's
  its parent's. The canvas draws it only on that level, a folder as its
  region's halo.
- A **row** is the tree's: a node or a **folder**, `{kind, path, parent, depth,
  label, segment, displayName, class, expandable}`. A folder row stands for a
  keyed container's field: its path is `parent/field`, the field alone under the
  root, which no node has; its children are the field's keyed nodes in walk
  order, and it sits where its first member would.
- A **level layout** (`LevelLayout`) is `{geometry, dragged, stale}`: a
  level's boxes and routes, ELK's output plus drags; the dragged block ids in
  drag order, the last drawn last; `stale` after a push until laid out again.
- A **hit** is a node or a face a query matches (`NodeHit`, `FaceHit`), with
  the matched ranges per field. A node is **marked** when it is a hit or owns a
  face hit; a closed assembly is **under** when it is not a hit and has marked
  nodes beneath it.
- A **region** is one of the four keyboard stops: `tree`, `canvas`, `detail`,
  `drawer`, each rooted at one focusable element. A **canvas region**
  (`Region` in `regions.ts`) is a keyed container's frame on the canvas,
  `{path, field, members}`, the path its folder's; the text says which.
- A **triple** is a timing `[anchor, D, phi]`, the core's `(a, Dₐ, Φₐ)`; in palette order each gets a
  **step**, 0 to 7 colored, 8 to 11 (`#9` to `#12`) the colored **extras**,
  12 on the shared neutral (`stepPair`). A legend line's **key** is
  `tripleKey`'s `"4,1,0"`, or `continuous`; its text is `legendText`'s, a
  deployment's `D₀ 2 · Φ₀ 1`, `1/50 s`, `A₀`, a build's `1/50 s · τ 0`, `A₂`. A **frames table** is a discrete
  primitive's or a rate scope's timing in the four frames of
  `deployment_overlay.md` answer 3, a row each (`FramesRow`). A wire's **class** is `scalar`,
  `flag`, `vector`, `struct` or `opaque`, from its terminal producer's kind.
- A **diagnostic** is a failure body's `diagnostics` row or any body's
  `warnings` row, one list (`diagnosticsOf`), errors first, keyed by its
  **index** there; its **severity** is `error` or `warning`. A loop's
  **chain** is its terminal pairs mapped onto declared wires, each **hop**
  `{level, wire, from, to}`; a closed child it passes through **continues**
  it. The **overlay** is what a level draws of them all (`Overlay`).
- A **focus** is one of three standing states that dim the canvas: the route,
  the focused diagnostic, the legend's line, one at a time in that order; what
  it lights is a `Lit`, `{blocks, wires, ports}`.
- An **analysis row** (`Row` in `analysis.ts`) is tagged by `kind`: `order`, a
  primitive with one `stage`'s ports, on stage 2 with its 1-based **position** in
  that group and its `edges`; `edge`, a feedthrough edge `{from, to}` under its
  consumer's row, **presumed**; `root-input`; and the timing tab's `anchor` (by
  its declaring scope, none for `A₀`), `scope` and `component`, each a
  `TimingTarget`. An **order badge** is a
  primitive's stage 2 position, or an assembly's as runs, "3–5, 8".
- An **event group** (`EventGroup`) is a declaring primitive's events, each an
  `EventRow` `{position, name, localized}`, the position its priority from 1,
  with its bundle; the events tab's one stop per component. The **count
  beneath** a node (`Beneath`) is its own events on a primitive, the sum over
  depth on an assembly.

## Built once on load

`loadModel(loaded)` builds, in order, the tree (`byPath`, `parent`,
`children` in walk order, `segment`, `membership`, `root`), the resolution
(`feeder`, the producer feeding each consumer face with its wire's level;
`fanout`, each producer face's consumers in wire order) and the feedthrough
edges. A structureless failure leaves all three, and `structure`, undefined;
any failure leaves `outputs` undefined. Levels and routes are computed on
demand from the resolution, never stored, and so is a deployment's timing
(`timing.ts`). A primitive root's level is the
root itself with a pin and a wire per face, built by `levelOf`, so the canvas has no
special case.

## Stores

The three lifetimes of `workspace.md` answer 9.

- **Per tab, in memory** (`session.svelte.ts`): the model; the open level by path,
  mirrored in the hash; `views` and `layouts`, each visited level's `View` and
  `LevelLayout` by path; the selection `{level, target}`; the expansion set of node
  and folder paths; `query`; `legendFocus`, a line's key, set by a legend line or a
  chart swatch; `diagnosticFocus`, an index; `route`, the standing route's origin
  face; the drawer's open state and tab; `sections`, the pane's open ones, every
  node's, kept by `load` (`setSection`); `focus`, the region holding the keyboard;
  `note`, the fallback note; `notice`, a one-off message until the next push, open,
  selection or trace; `help`, mirrored by the hash `#help`. A node expanding, by
  `toggleExpanded` or by `expand`, brings its folders. Raw state throughout: each
  change replaces the value, so no deep proxy wraps the model.
  - `load` of a first model, or of one after a refusal, resets all the rest:
    the root open, the root and its folders expanded, nothing else kept. The
    drawer opens on `diagnostics` only when the body carries a diagnostic
    (`opensDrawer`), a warning alone leaving it closed though counted.
    `load(undefined)`, a refusal, unloads.
  - `load` of a replacement passes the state through `rekey` and marks every
    kept layout `stale`; the legend's focus goes through `rekeyFocus` (an
    anchor followed by key and scope), the route through `rekeyRoute`, kept
    while the new model's node at its path declares the face and `routeOf`
    resolves it, else cleared with a notice, and the shell
    recomputes it; the focused diagnostic is dropped, an index having no
    key; the drawer keeps its state and opens on `diagnostics` when the new
    model carries one.
  - In both, a hash naming another level of the new model wins, its view
    dropped and the note cleared; then the level's ancestors expand and the
    hash is replaced. Under the help page `load` leaves `help` and `#help`
    standing, and the entry below is read when the page closes.
  - `open` pushes a history entry unless the hash already names the level;
    `climb` is `open` of the parent. `openFromHistory`, on `popstate`, reads
    `#help` (`isHelpHash`) as the help page and any other hash as its level,
    through `show` only when the level changes; it pushes nothing. `show`
    selects the ancestor's child on a move to a strict ancestor
    (`childOnPath`), forward and a tree double-click included. `select`
    replaces the hash and expands a node's ancestors. The hash is `#level=`
    and the path through `encodeURIComponent`, `%2F` put back; the root has
    none.
  - The history depth: this load's entries carry `{load, depth}` in
    `history.state`; `open` pushes one deeper and sets `top`; a `null` state,
    from a hash link or a typed hash, is stamped one deeper than the entry
    left. `back` (Shift-`h`) acts above depth 0 and `forward` (Shift-`l`)
    below `top`, so neither leaves the page.
  - The help page: the module reads `#help` at load. `openHelp` pushes
    `#help` one deeper; `closeHelp` hides the page, then goes back when the
    entry below is this load's (`depth > 0`), else replaces the hash with the
    open level's. `toggleHelp` is `?`.
  - `escape`: the help page, else the route, else the focused diagnostic,
    else the legend's focus, else a non-empty query, else the selection.
    `trace(origin)` sets the route, or clears it for the standing origin
    (`sameFace`); `toggleLegendFocus` and `toggleDiagnosticFocus` clear on a
    second click. `open`, `climb`, `select` and the history leave the route
    and the diagnostic focus standing; the shell clears the legend's focus
    whenever the mode is `tier`.
- **The push's rekeying** (`rekey.ts`), by answer 9's keys. A level that is
  no longer a level falls to its longest segment-aligned prefix that is, the
  root at worst, with `note` reading `` `<old>` is not in this model; showing
  `<new>` `` ("the root" for the root). A selected node is placed on its old
  level when that survives, else on its parent. A view survives only while
  the level's set of block ids is unchanged (`sameBlocks`), and the fallback
  level's view is dropped so it fits. The query is kept whole. A model
  without a tree rekeys to the root, everything dropped but the query.
- **Per tab, the connection** (`live.svelte.ts`): `state` (see "The live
  tab"), `text`, the raw descriptor last received, and `attempt`, the
  reconnects since the loss. Only `src/live/socket.ts` sets them.
- **Per browser** (`prefs.svelte.ts`): one local storage key,
  `redstone-inspector/prefs`, holding `{version: 1, sizes: {tree, detail,
  drawer}, collapsed: {tree, detail}, mode, labels, order}`, read once at
  load through `parsePrefs`. It gives the defaults (280, 360, 200, nothing
  collapsed, `tier`, labels and order off) on a missing, malformed or
  other-version entry, keeps the default for a missing or mistyped field, so
  a new field needs no version bump, and clamps a size (tree 160 to 640,
  detail 240 to 800, drawer 80 to 640). Written on every change, each move
  of a drag included; never rekeyed.

## The canvas

`Canvas.svelte` takes `model`, `path`, `mode`, `labels`, `order`, `selected`,
`view`, `layout`, `hits`, `lit`, `focus`, `focusKey`, `legendFocus`,
`overlay`, `routeDrawn` and `focused`, and calls `onopen(path)`,
`onselect(target)` (`undefined` for a background click), `onview(view)`,
`onlayout(geometry, dragged)`, `onlegend(key)`, `onbadge(index)` and
`ontrace(face)`. The caller decides what a click selects and passes back the
target only when it was made on the drawn level. The shell keys the canvas on
the model (`{#key model}`), so each model gets a fresh one.

On each `path` it builds and sizes the level with its regions and type
labels. A held layout not `stale` is drawn at once, `data-ready` never absent;
otherwise `relayout` clears `data-ready`, runs ELK, puts the layout's dragged
blocks back with their wires re-routed, reports `onlayout`, sets `data-ready`
and restores the view or fits. `undrag(all)`, `x` and `X`, runs it on
`dragged` less its last id, or none, in the current view; a newer run or path
drops an older result (`run`). `view` and `layout` are read with `untrack`; a
level change clears the hover and closes the menu. The draw order is answer 9's: regions, casings (the
route's first), wires with struct cores, dots, blocks with ports and halos,
type labels, `g.overlay`, `g.order-badges`, `g.route-pills`. `<defs>` holds one arrowhead per wire color, in
user units, and one `<symbol>` per glyph.

What the records do not fix, or where the page differs:

  name `11px`, a pin's label `12px`, type labels and badges `9px`; the display

  key, a primitive root's the model's label, else its segment), its second line and each row's input and output

## The frame

`App.svelte` shows `Landing` until a model loads, then `Frame` with the panes
as snippets: the tree and the detail pane at the sides, full height, with
their collapse buttons; between them the breadcrumb, the canvas area, the
drawer and the status bar. A structureless failure shows an empty tree and
`pre.outcome` in the canvas area; a refusal unloads to the landing's
sentence. The title is `<label> – Redstone inspector`, or `Redstone
inspector` alone. The live tab's landing has a status bar while `connected`
or `lost`. The help page shows over either, which stays mounted and `inert`.

What the records do not fix, or where the page differs:

- **The tree.** A click selects a node on its parent's level, the root on
  the open level, a folder on its parent's. A selection made elsewhere or by
  a key scrolls its row into view; a row's own click does not. The search
  field is the `search` snippet, so it belongs to the tree region. `hit` and
  `.hits` mark the query's nodes, else the legend focus's (`legendMarks`). The

## The keyboard

`App.svelte`'s window `keydown`, as built from `workspace.md` answer 11.
Cmd-O and Ctrl-O come first. The help page then takes `?`, Escape and `j k d u
gg G`, every other key the browser's, Tab included. Otherwise Tab comes next,
from the search field too, cycling only with a model, then Cmd-K and Ctrl-K,
from a text field too; every other key is ignored in a text field, where the search field has its own Down, Up, Enter
and Escape and the filter its Escape. Ctrl or Meta leaves a key to the browser but Cmd-K, Ctrl-K, Cmd-O
and Ctrl-O; with Alt only Alt-Up and Alt-Backspace act, climbing. `keyName`
names every letter by Shift, Caps Lock aside. `?`, Cmd-O and the help page's
keys act without a model, every other row only with one. `help.spec.ts` counts
this table's rows against `table.keys` (`KEYS` in `help.ts`): change both.

`z` and `g` are prefixes (`pressPrefix`), shown by `span.prefix`. A pending one
comes before the keys below, Tab outside a text field included, and drops a
second key it does not know. A `z` is held to its `keyup`, heard wherever the
focus is; a window `blur` and an Alt key (`endsHold`) end a hold, and a key with
Ctrl or Meta, a refusal and the help page opening or closing any prefix.

## The live tab

`src/live/socket.ts`, as `frontend/PROTOCOL.md` and `live_session.md` answers
4 to 6 say. `connect(onDescriptor)`, called once on mount, opens
`ws://<host>/ws` at the page's origin (`wss` under `https:`). On `open` it
sends `helloMessage(location.search)`, the channel `""` without a `tab`
parameter, resets `attempt` and marks `connected`. A text frame
`classifyFrame` reads as a descriptor is kept as `live.text` and handed to
`onDescriptor`; anything else is dropped. The shell parses the text with no
file name and loads it as a drop's. A close before any `open` is the hosted
copy: `hosted`, no retry, nothing shown. A close after one marks `lost`,
waits `backoff(attempt)` (1, 2, 4 s, capped at 30 s) and opens again. No
keepalive. `sendOpenLocation(file, line)` sends while `connected` and
returns true, else returns false.

| `live.state` | `.connection` |
|---|---|
| `undefined`, before the handshake resolves | absent |
| `hosted` | absent |
| `connected`, no descriptor received | `connected, waiting for inspect` (`inspect` in `code`) |
| `connected`, a descriptor received | `connected` |
| `lost` | `disconnected, retrying` |

## The browser checks

`npm run e2e` runs Playwright over `e2e/` against `vite preview` on port 4319
serving `dist/`, so `npm run build` runs first. Two projects, `chromium` and
`firefox`, both blocking in CI. The clipboard checks skip under Firefox.

stands; `press` presses a key and waits a frame; `live` mocks the socket.

check runs the hosted copy. Each file's header comment names its records,
its test names what they pin.
